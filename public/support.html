Little Sisyphus

Support

Little Sisyphus is a focus timer. Choose how long you want to concentrate, and Sisyphus climbs for as long as you stay with it.

Signing in

Enter your email and we send you a six-digit code. There is no password to remember. If the code does not arrive, check your spam folder, then request another after a minute.

Leaving a session early

If you leave the app mid-climb you have five seconds to return. After that you can pay to keep the elevation you earned, or forfeit it. Forfeiting resets your lifetime total to zero.

The ranking

Climbers are ordered by total time focused. Your climber name is public; your email is never shown to anyone.

Deleting your account

Open the account screen in the app and choose Delete account. This removes your entry from the ranking and erases your data permanently.
