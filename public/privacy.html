Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 August 2026

Little Sisyphus is a focus timer. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what control you have over it. We collect as little as the app can function with.

What we collect

DataWhyVisible to others
Email addressTo sign you in and keep your progress with you across devicesNo
Climber nameShown on the rankingYes — publicly
Total time focusedDetermines your position on the rankingYes — publicly

That is the complete list. We do not collect your name, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers for advertising, or any usage analytics.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or behavioural tracking
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with third parties
  • No third-party trackers of any kind in the app

Who processes your data

  • Supabase — stores your account and the ranking
  • Brevo — delivers your sign-in code by email
  • Expo — delivers app updates

Each of these processes data only on our instruction.

Payments

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple and Google. We never see or store your card details. We are told only that a purchase succeeded.

How long we keep it

Your account and total remain until you delete your account. Sign-in codes expire within an hour and are not retained.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it entirely. To delete your account and all associated data, use the delete option in the app under your account screen, or email us at the address below. Deletion is permanent and removes your entry from the ranking.

If you are in the UK or the EU, you also have the right to complain to your data protection regulator.

Children

Little Sisyphus is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and, where the change affects you, tell you in the app.

Contact
